# What's new in Witchspace

Changes players will notice, newest first, in plain words. Commanders who've
played before get the newest section as a message from Witchspace when they next
dock (src/game/changelog.ts reads this file).

Adding to it: put a line under today's date with any change a player would
notice. Make a new `## 9 November 2026` heading (day, month in full, year) if
there isn't one for today. Write one line per change, starting with `- `. Say
what's different, not how it was done. Credit whoever reported it.

## 9 November 2026

- Important radio messages show for a moment in the middle of the screen, then shrink away into the radio's corner, where they're kept. (Mark)
- The trip computer says what the next leg needs when you arrive: where the next jump goes, how far, and whether to refuel first.
- Trails are said plainly. A trail step's signal is named "Trail signal" in gold. Finding one says which trail, how many steps you've found and where it leads next. The galaxy chart marks each trail's next system and lists your trails. (Mark)
- Finding a trail step no longer replaces a course you've already set, for a contract say. It tells you where the trail goes instead.
- Every signal's log is kept in your messages, to read again when you're docked. (a player on the ideas board)
- The job card and the course you plot now take the same route. The card could say one system while the course went to another, which sent you round in circles. (Mark)
- Running dark past the police now gets one hail and one countdown. If the Viper that hailed you docks, the next one in sight carries the hail on, instead of starting a new 15 seconds.
- The jump countdown names where you're going.
- Routes are planned as far as your tank reaches, not a fixed 7 light years.
- The local chart shows your whole range on a wide screen, and draws your next jump.
- "Find a system" no longer covers the chart's title on a phone held sideways.
- The docking computer waits while the station's rings are red, and no longer flips between holding and going in at a busy station.
- Police and militia never launch out of a slot you've been cleared into: they start from the far side of the station instead.
- Answers to a police hail are easier to see and tap on a phone.
- The market reads "computers are" and "food is".

## 7 November 2026

- Shooting down another commander is titled "Commander down at" the system, in your flight log.

## 6 November 2026

- Dogfights with other commanders are kept in your flight log.
- The crosshair sits on the nose, where fixed lasers fire. (maniacminer on Stardot)
- Roll assist works only while you're flying in, and never with the BBC keys. Keyboard controls ignore scrolling, and one Escape or F1 closes the controls card. (maniacminer on Stardot)

## 5 November 2026

- A shared clip's link preview has a line written from the fight.

## 4 November 2026

- Sharing to Facebook from a phone goes through the phone's own share sheet.
- Phones get the share buttons too, once a clip has its own page.

## 3 November 2026

- Clips upload from an iPhone again.
- The share sheet has one main button and one link.
- The flight log tells you the truth when a phone's storage misbehaves.
