Privacy

Witchspace keeps as little about you as it can. No adverts, no tracking cookies, and nothing is sold.

Playing without an account

Your commander is saved in your own browser and never leaves it. Nothing about you is sent to us.

If you sign in

  • What we keep: your email address, your callsign, and a copy of your commander (the cloud save), so you can carry on from any device.
  • Why: your email is only used to send you sign-in links. We don't send newsletters or anything else unless you ask for them.
  • If you let the companies contact you: only if you switch it on (press A, then Let the companies contact me). For notifications we keep the address your browser gives us for them, which identifies nothing but that browser; for email we use your sign-in address. Messages come at most twice a week, in character, when a special assignment is waiting, and every email has a one-click stop. Switch it off at any time, and deleting your account removes it.
  • Security: sign-in links work once and expire after 20 minutes. Links and sessions are stored only as one-way hashes.
  • Deleting it: press A on the title screen, then Delete account. That removes your email, callsign, cloud save, sessions and contact settings straight away. Your commander stays in your browser.

Shared space

When you fly in shared space, your ship's position, callsign and rank are sent to the other commanders in your star system while you're there. Nothing is kept after you leave.

The ideas board

To give each idea one vote per person without accounts, your browser keeps a random id. To stop abuse we keep a one-way hash of your network address, never the address itself. What you post is public.

Who handles it

The website is hosted by Vercel. Shared space, the ideas board and accounts run on Cloudflare. Sign-in emails, and company messages if you ask for them, are sent by Resend. Notifications go through your browser's own push service (Apple, Google or Mozilla), which sees only that a message arrived, not what it says. Images and videos are served by Cloudinary. Cloudflare's Turnstile checks that you're a person when you sign in or post an idea. The website's fonts come from Google Fonts, which sees your browser's address when it loads them.
