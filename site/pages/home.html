WITCHSPACE

Trade, fight and explore a galaxy of glowing wireframe ships, right in your browser. A love letter to Elite (1984).

Play now Get involved

Remember the first time you docked?

Witchspace keeps the soul of the original (crisp vector ships, hidden-line wireframes, that heart-in-mouth docking) and renders it the way a BBC Micro never could. Press M any time to drop back to authentic 1984 mode, 256×192 pixels in white on black.

Neo-vector

Glowing lines, bloom, explosions that shatter hulls into tumbling panels. Light enough to run on almost anything.

Fly it your way

Mouse aim with W/S/A/D, the classic keyboard-only scheme, a gamepad, the touch surface of a Magic Mouse, or touch and tilt on a phone.

Classic or Modern

One life and GAME OVER, as it was in 1984. Or Modern rules, with flight assists and a little more forgiveness.

Built by its community

Ships, worlds and missions are open content. Design them by hand or with your favourite AI assistant.

What you can do today

Witchspace is an early prototype, growing in the open.

  • Launch from a spinning Coriolis station, and dock by hand or with a docking computer and the waltz
  • Trade the classic seventeen goods in living markets. Start, as ever, with 100 credits
  • Explore 2,048 star systems across eight galaxies, just where you remember them, each with a named station
  • Hyperspace between systems, and use the in-system jump drive to cross them
  • Share space with traders, police and pirates. Collect bounties and climb from Harmless towards Elite
  • Fit pulse, beam or military lasers front, rear and sides, and look around while you fly
  • Lock on and launch missiles, and buy an ECM for when they're coming the other way
  • Scoop cargo from wrecks, and skim a star for fuel if you can stand the heat
  • Live with the law: local records, docking clearance, police scans, fines, and surrender when it all goes wrong
  • Switch between neo-vector and 1984 mode at any moment
  • Play on a phone or tablet with touch controls, or tilt to steer

Coming next: more ships, missions and the road to multiplayer. See the design and have your say.

Fair by design

Free and open source

Code under GPL-3.0, content under CC BY-SA 4.0. Forks stay open, and contributions stay credited.

No crypto, no pay-to-win

Nothing in Witchspace can be bought with real money. Everything is earned by playing or by contributing.

Decided together

The community votes on what gets built next. Designs are written in the open before they're coded.
