WITCHSPACE

Trade, fight and explore a galaxy of glowing wireframe ships, right in your browser. A love letter to Elite (1984).

Play now Get involved

Remember the first time you docked?

Witchspace keeps the soul of the original (crisp vector ships, hidden-line wireframes, that heart-in-mouth docking) and renders it the way a BBC Micro never could. Press M any time to drop back to authentic 1984 mode, 256×192 pixels in white on black.

Neo-vector

Glowing lines, bloom, explosions that shatter hulls into tumbling panels. Light enough to run on almost anything.

Fly it your way

Mouse aim with W/S/A/D, the classic keyboard-only scheme, a gamepad, or the touch surface of a Magic Mouse.

Classic or Modern

One life and GAME OVER, as it was in 1984. Or Modern rules, with flight assists and a little more forgiveness.

Built by its community

Ships, worlds and missions are open content. Design them by hand or with your favourite AI assistant.

What you can do today

Witchspace is an early prototype, growing in the open.

  • Launch from a spinning Coriolis station
  • Dock by hand, or buy a docking computer and enjoy the waltz
  • Fight the police, and live with the consequences
  • Upgrade to gimballed lasers
  • Watch your wreckage tumble and your cargo burn up in the atmosphere
  • Switch between neo-vector and 1984 mode at any moment

Coming next: a galaxy of 2,048 star systems, living markets, witchspace jumps and the law. See the design.

Fair by design

Free and open source

Code under GPL-3.0, content under CC BY-SA 4.0. Forks stay open, and contributions stay credited.

No crypto, no pay-to-win

Nothing in Witchspace can be bought with real money. Everything is earned by playing or by contributing.

Decided together

The community votes on what gets built next. Designs are written in the open before they're coded.
