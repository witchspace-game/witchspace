Two looks, one key

1984 is always one keypress away

Neo-vector brings bloom, colour and shattering explosions. Press M at any moment and you're back in 1984: white lines on black, sharpened for modern screens. Drag to compare.

Neo-vector 1984

Combat

Dogfights with weight

Same physics for everyone: pitch, roll and no drift, just like 1984. Pirates hunt traders, police answer crimes, and your laser runs hot if you hold the trigger too long.

  • Pulse, beam and military lasers on four mounts
  • Homing missiles, ECM and the energy bomb
  • Bounties, and a combat rating from Harmless to Elite

Trade

A market that moves

Every station runs a real exchange. Other traders' deals move the price, stations post orders when they run short, and news of a famine three jumps away is worth money, if you get there first.

  • Candlestick charts, an order book and market orders
  • Supply chains, world events and news that travels
  • A back room for contraband, if you dare

Explore

2,048 systems, right where you left them

Every galaxy is generated just as it was in 1984, so Lave, Diso and Leesti are where you remember. Now there's more to find there: ringed worlds and moons, survey data to sell, and signals that lead to derelicts and their logs.

  • Survey planets, moons and stars, and sell the data far away
  • Derelicts, beacons and wreck fields, the same for everyone
  • Hyperspace, in-system jumps and galactic hyperdrives
  • Fuel scoops: skim a star for fuel, scoop cargo from wrecks
  • Pirates, police and traders, each with somewhere to be

Your ship

Kit out your Cobra

Dock, trade and refit with your ship on the pad, turning it to see your upgrades. Laser louvres and missile hatches show on the hull, so a sharp-eyed pilot can tell what you're carrying.

  • Docking computer, with that waltz
  • Cargo bay, extra energy, escape pod and more
  • The law: records, scans, fines, and surrender when it goes wrong

Your game, your way

Pick it up anywhere

Mouse and keyboard

Aim with the mouse and fly with W, A, S, D. Trackpads and Magic Mouse swipes work too.

Classic keyboard

The keyboard-only scheme, close to the BBC original, for purists.

Gamepad

Sticks for pitch, roll and yaw; triggers for speed. Launch and dock from the sofa.

Phone and tablet

Touch controls, or tilt to steer. Turn your phone sideways and fly.

Classic or Modern rules

One life and GAME OVER, as in 1984. Or Modern: flight assists, insurance, and a little forgiveness.

Nothing to install

It runs in any modern browser, on almost any machine. Your commander saves as you play.

Built in the open

A galaxy made by its players

Ships are small JSON files, worlds and missions are open content, and the design is written down before it's built, so everyone can have a say.

Design a ship

Draw one by hand or with your favourite AI assistant. Drop the file in and it flies.
