Ship editor

Shape a ship from points: the editor mirrors them and makes the faces, so it's always a sound hull. Start from a ship in the fleet or a plain wedge, give it a name, a maker and a reason to exist, and hand it in. Your drawing is kept in this browser as you go.

Drag a point to move it. Hollow points are mirror images. Grey ones are inside the hull, so they don't change its shape.
