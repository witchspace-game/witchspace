About Witchspace

Witchspace is an open-source, browser-based space trading game, and an unofficial, non-commercial tribute to Elite.

Thank you, Ian and David

In 1984, Ian Bell and David Braben wrote Elite for the BBC Micro while they were undergraduates at Cambridge, and Acornsoft published it. They fitted 3D wireframe space flight, eight procedurally generated galaxies and an open-ended trading game into about 22KB of memory. It invented a genre, and for many of us it was the moment computers became worlds.

The ship names in Witchspace (Cobra Mk III, Viper, Coriolis and the rest) are a deliberate tribute to their work. Everything else is new: the code, the ship designs, the text and the art were created from scratch for this project.

Elite is a trademark of Frontier Developments plc. Witchspace is not affiliated with or endorsed by Frontier Developments, Ian Bell or David Braben.

Kindred spirits

  • Oolite: two decades of proof that a fan-built, open-source tribute can thrive.
  • Mark Moxon's fully documented Elite source: an extraordinary labour of love explaining how the original works.

No crypto, no pay-to-win

Witchspace is a non-commercial fan project, and nothing in the game can ever be bought with real money: no tokens, no NFTs, no paid credits, ships or advantages. Everything is earned by playing or by contributing. If running costs ever need covering, it will be through transparent donations with every penny public, and supporters get thanks, never power.
