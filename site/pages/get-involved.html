Get involved

Witchspace is built by the people who love it. Every kind of contribution counts, not just code. In time, contributing is also how you earn the right to run a station.

Design a ship

Ships are small JSON files: vertices, faces, stats and a glow colour. Drop one in and it appears in the game. A checker tells you if anything's wrong.

Write the galaxy

Star systems, lore, missions and rumours. Dry, wry and a little lawless. There's a worldbuilding guide to keep the tone right.

Build legends

Rare easter-egg ships that tip their hat to great science fiction. Evoke the idea, never copy the design.

Playtest

Fly it, break it, tell us how it feels. Good bug reports are gold.

Code

TypeScript and three.js. Flight, combat, the economy, multiplayer, tools. There's plenty to build.

Shape the design

Read the design documents, argue for your ideas, and vote on what comes next.

Bring your own AI

Witchspace is built to work with AI assistants such as Claude, ChatGPT, Grok and Cursor. Every content type has a strict schema, and an AGENTS.md guide tells any assistant how to contribute well.

Ask yours to "design a pirate gunship for Witchspace" and it will write against the schema and check its work with the validator. Please tick the "AI-assisted" box on your pull request, and play-test what you submit: you're the author.

Also on the way: a Witchspace MCP server so assistants can validate, preview and balance-check content directly. A machine-readable summary of the project lives at /llms.txt.

How to start

  1. Find the project on GitHub and read the contributing guide.
  2. Play the game, then pick something you'd love to see: a ship, a system, a fix.
  3. Say hello in GitHub Discussions, or email hello@witchspace.org.uk.

Everyone taking part agrees to the code of conduct. Problems can be reported privately to conduct@witchspace.org.uk.
