Get involved

Build it with us

Witchspace is built in the open by the people who love it. Most of what it needs isn't code: ships, characters, stories, playtesting and good arguments about design. In time, contributing is also how you earn the right to run a station.

Design a ship

Every ship is a small file of points, faces and a glow colour. Browse the fleet, suggest improvements on any ship's page, or draw your own. A ship editor in the browser is on the way, so you won't need GitHub for this.

Write the galaxy

Saloon regulars, company people, trail stories, beacon logs, rumours. Dry, wry and a little lawless, and every story has people in it. Start with the worldbuilding guide.

Build a Legend

Rare easter-egg ships that tip their hat to great science fiction. Evoke the idea, never copy the design.

Playtest

Fly it, break it, tell us how it feels. Press P for a clean photo of what you saw. Good bug reports are gold.

Shape the design

Every feature is designed in the open before it's built. Read the designs, argue for your ideas, and vote on what comes next.

Code

TypeScript and three.js: flight, combat, the economy, the shared galaxy, tools. There's plenty to build.

Bring your own AI

Witchspace is built to work with AI assistants such as Claude, ChatGPT, Grok and Cursor. Every content type has a strict schema, and an AGENTS.md guide tells any assistant how to contribute well.

Ask yours to "design a pirate gunship for Witchspace" and it will write against the schema and check its work with the validator. Please tick the "AI-assisted" box on your pull request, and play-test what you submit: you're the author.

Also on the way: a Witchspace MCP server, one web address that lets your assistant validate, preview and balance-check a ship, read every design and help-wanted task, and look up any system. A machine-readable summary of the project lives at /llms.txt.

How to start

  1. Play the game, then pick something from help wanted, or something you'd love to see.
  2. Say which in Discussions, or email hello@witchspace.org.uk, and we'll help you get going.
  3. If you're comfortable with GitHub, the contributing guide has the details. If you're not, you don't need it.

Everyone taking part agrees to the code of conduct. Problems can be reported privately to conduct@witchspace.org.uk.
