# Design: the Shrike

*Design, 24 October 2026. On hold: the sketches didn't work (boxy, no clear job), and the Shrike waits for a place in Solo & Sons' line-up ([ships, classes and makers](ships-and-classes.md)).*

The second concept from **Solo & Sons** ([the Falcons, a Legend](legends-the-falcon.md)): Iver Solo and his four sons, a yard hidden in the lawless systems, sheltering the forty-one from the *Ninefold*. Their ships are named for birds of prey. The shrike is the bird that pins its prey before it eats.

## Leaked plans

Halloran Sisters show their concepts off. Solo & Sons can't: the yard is hiding from Wexley-Yorozu. So their concepts reach the site as **leaked plans**, *"drawings recovered from a Falcon's wreck near Riedquat"*, half-redacted, in pirate red. A different voice for the same kind of page.

## The ship

- **Bram's ship** (the eldest; mad). He flew escort for the *Hold Fast* the day it left the *Ninefold*, and he takes Wexley-Yorozu cargo first. The Shrike is built to stop a ship, not to destroy it: the Falcons' code is to disable, take what they came for, and leave you alive.
- **The clamp.** At the nose, something that grips a ship's hull and drains its drive, so it can't run or jump while the Shrike's crew take the cargo. It's how disabling and capture ([law and travel](law-and-travel.md#5-damage-disabling-and-capture)) comes into the game, for pirates first and later for anyone with the kit.
- **Size and feel:** a short, heavy interceptor, about 45 m long and 38 m across (a Cobra is 50 by 68): deep wedge hull, a raised cockpit, two drive pods, small swept vanes to shed heat like the Falcon's. Pirate red.

## The sketches

Three ideas for the nose, rendered in the game's own style (three-quarter, from above beside a Cobra, from the side):

- **A, hammer nose:** an armoured block with a short two-pronged clamp. Reads as a gunship.
- **B, hooked beak:** a long spar from the nose that curves down to a hook, like a shrike's bill. The most distinctive silhouette, and the most like the bird.
- **C, talons:** two pincer arms either side of the nose. Reads as a crab more than a bird.

**Recommendation: B.** It's the one you'd know from any angle, and the hook is the clamp. The hull in all three is boxy for a sketch; whichever is chosen gets refined (a slimmer hull, the hook's joints, the drive pods).

## Also in the yard's drawers

Each son builds to suit himself. Later concepts, if the Shrike lands well:

- **The Kestrel** (Kit, glad): a sprinter, the fastest thing in a straight line (the Dragonfly out-turns everything; the Kestrel out-runs it). A reason for races and time trials.
- **The Hearth** (Jory, sad): a quiet blockade-runner for food and medicine to the forty-one, with pods that drop away to drift dark if a Marshal comes close. Named for home, not a bird.
- **The Rook** (Tobin, bad): heavier, uglier, better armed, built with Vorg Industries' money, and his brothers hate it. The first Solo ship you'd want to see destroyed.
