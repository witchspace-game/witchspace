# Audio credits

## blue-danube.mp3

*An der schönen, blauen Donau* ("The Blue Danube"), Op. 314, by Johann Strauss II (1866), arranged for wind ensemble. Performed by the **United States Marine Band, "The President's Own"**.

- Source: [Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:%22An_der_sch%C3%B6nen,_blauen_Donau%22_performed_by_the_U.S._Marine_Band.mp3)
- Licence: **public domain**. A performance recorded by the U.S. Marine Band is a work of the United States federal government, which is not subject to copyright in the U.S. (17 U.S.C. § 105). The composition itself is long out of copyright.

Used in neo-vector mode while the docking computer is engaged. 1984 mode uses a chiptune arrangement synthesised in code (`src/audio/sound.ts`).

## semper-fidelis.mp3

*Semper Fidelis* (1888) by John Philip Sousa, from the band's own edition of his original score (edited by Col. Jason K. Fettig and members of the band). Performed by the **United States Marine Band, "The President's Own"**, conducted by Lt. Col. Jason K. Fettig: track 34 of *The Complete Marches of John Philip Sousa, Vol. 2 (1883–1889)*, 2016.

- Source: [Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Sousa%27s_%22Semper_Fidelis%22_-_United_States_Marine_Band_(2016).mp3), from [the band's site](https://www.marineband.marines.mil/Audio-Resources/The-Complete-Marches-of-John-Philip-Sousa/)
- Licence: **public domain**: a work of the US federal government (17 U.S.C. § 105); the march was published in 1888, and the band says its Sousa editions are in the public domain.

Played when the Co-operative Marshals come through a witchspace rift. Sousa led the Marine Band from 1880 to 1892.

## witches-sabbath.mp3

*Songe d'une nuit du sabbat* ("Dream of a Witches' Sabbath"), the fifth movement of the *Symphonie fantastique* (1830) by Hector Berlioz, transcribed for band by Lt. Col. Jack T. Kline, the band's director. Performed by the **United States Marine Band, "The President's Own"**, from the album *From Fife and Drum*. The first 2 minutes 32 seconds of the movement.

- Source: [Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:A_Dream_of_a_Witches%27_Sabbath_-_transcribed_by_Lt._Col._Jack_T._Cline_-_U.S._Marine_Band.ogg) (its MP3 version)
- Licence: **public domain**: a work of the US federal government, transcribed by the band's own director; Berlioz died in 1869.

Played when the Falcon's hull is read.
